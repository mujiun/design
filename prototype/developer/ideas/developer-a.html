Overview

Understand what you need to do based on your goals and the integration path you choose.

Select your integration path

Integrate WhatsApp API directly into your own systems. Best for businesses building in-house messaging — e.g. a retailer sending order updates, a bank handling customer support, or an airline managing booking notifications.
Build solutions that serve other businesses. Best for SaaS platforms and technology companies — e.g. Twilio, MessageBird, or engagement tools offering WhatsApp as part of their product.
