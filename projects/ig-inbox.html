Before / After

Inconsistent product experiences caused by the lack of a shared framework and fast-moving execution across teams.

Unified Page Information Architecture

Defined a modular framework to unify and simplify experiences across five tools, including consolidating two tools into one to reduce configuration complexity. This will ease adoption and reduce maintenance cost.



Unified Setup Flow

Standardized the setup flow across tools to simplify onboarding and improve adoption, creating a predictable editing experience across tools.


Beyond Modular System

Beyond building modular systems across messaging tools, we launched a cross-org workstream to improve tool discovery and adoption across the end-to-end journey. Connecting the dots was key to driving tangible results.
