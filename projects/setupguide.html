Context

Strategic partner plays a critical role for ecosystem growth. Over millions of businesses use WhatsApp on partner platforms like AiSensy, Twilio to manage their customers. However, after signup, only 9% of businesses became the paid businesses, indicating the adoption an $XM revenue gap.

Discover Problems

Integration with partner platforms requires multiple and sequential setup steps. Most users prefer step-by-step guidance to complete the setup. However, research shows that 90% of WhatsApp partners offer hub-and-spoke guide, creating integration challenges.

Clarify Ambiguity

To build the right solution, we needed to answer two questions:

  • What does the setup path look like? Steps, sequence, and required tools.
  • Who should build the guidance? Meta or partner platforms?

Understanding Gap for Setup Path

WhatsApp integration involves setup steps owned by 5+ product teams and spread across multiple Meta and partner platforms. As a result, no one had a clear view of the end-to-end setup journey.



To drive clarity on setup steps, I led cross-functional efforts with internal and partner teams to map the end-to-end setup journey.



From research, I've identified 3 problem layers that drove adoption down, and one framework to solve it. 3 problems I found:

  • Friction shifted, not solved: Teams optimized their own stage, pushing frictions to other stages.
  • Scattered drop-offs: Users dropped across many steps, so no single fix could lift conversion.
  • Gap after onboarding: Meta’s support ended at onboarding, but adoption happened after.
