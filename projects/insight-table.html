Client Account & Insight Table

Information Architecture, Strategy Pivot
Overview
Problem
Partners couldn't track or support their clients when they got stuck — supporting them meant a client call or a request to Meta.
Solution
One table to manage client accounts and track four product-usage statuses, from onboarding to spending — so partners can track, diagnose, and act in one place.
My Role
  • Led design strategy and the scope pivot
  • Partnered with 3 engineers and a PM through the full cycle
  • Delivered the table design and a component library in HTML
Impact
  • 800+ partners track and support clients in one place
  • 4 teams co-build on one tool, not 4
  • Cut the design process from 3 months to 1 month
Context

Partners sit between WhatsApp and their clients. WhatsApp offers the API; 800+ partners build their platforms with it; 500k end businesses use those platforms to engage their customers on WhatsApp.

Discover Problems

Partners couldn't track and support their clients when they got stuck.

  • Why it matters — partners drive 80% of WhatsApp business messaging revenue.
  • High effort — to support a client, partners had to rely on a client call or a request to Meta.
Define Scope

Pivoted the Scope

The team's initial idea was a dashboard to track a single onboarding status — it only solved one task and part of the workflow. I asked two questions to reframe the problem and pivoted the scope from a single step to a broader operational need.

What the Table Tracks

The insight table pairs 2 account assets with 4 product-usage tasks, so a partner can see a client's full status at a glance.

  • Account assets — business account, phone number(s)
  • Product-usage tasks — onboarding status, business verification, payment, message sent

Measured on partner usage, completion rate of the four tasks, and revenue.

Design Exploration

Rather than build a new surface, I pivoted to build on the existing table and explored three directions — then landed on the flat list.

Idea #1 — Two Tabs

Represents the account structure and moves fast by adding a single tab. Discarded: adds maintenance cost and friction — 95% of clients have a single account and number.

Idea #2 — Grouped Table

Surfaces onboarding issues first — the team's top priority and the biggest visibility gap for partners. Discarded: a big change that risks adoption, leaves no flex for other task tracking, is costly to change later, and has low revenue impact.

Idea #3 — Flat List, Chosen

Meets all operational needs: four statuses across the full client lifecycle, all teams building on one table, landing revenue and long-term impact.

Column Explorations

Within the flat list, I tested how to represent phone numbers and dates.

  • Registered date under phone number — discarded; multiple numbers in one column got overwhelming.
  • Nested phone numbers under account — discarded; added friction and was unnecessary for the 95% with a single account and number.
Iteration & Handoff

Default View — 4 Columns

The table shows four columns by default.

  • Business + Verification stays visible so partners keep their existing mental model.
  • Phone number + Onboarding status leads, as that's where clients get stuck and need support fastest.

Supported the Full Workflow

  • Track — one default view to start; filter and sort to focus.
  • Diagnose — status pills show the overview, hover for details.
  • Act — action items to address each issue.

Handoff

I highlighted four changes between the existing and expanded table:

  • Remove Resources card
  • Relocate search bar
  • Add column controls
  • Add 4 new columns

I delivered the table design and a component library in HTML. Using Claude Code for design exploration and alignment cut the full process from 3 months to 1 month.

Impact & Learning

Impact

  • 800+ partners can track and support clients from onboarding to spending in one place.
  • 4 teams co-build on one tool instead of four separate ones.
  • The design-to-handoff process shrank from 3 months to 1 month.

Future Idea

Summary cards for high-level stats and quick actions could surface the highest-priority issues without making partners scan every row. I deprioritized it for now — limited space, added distraction, and alignment needed across teams.

Learning

Reframing scope from a single task to the operational workflow is what unlocked impact. Pivoting from a one-off dashboard to a shared insight table let four teams build on one surface — moving the numbers more than any single screen could.
